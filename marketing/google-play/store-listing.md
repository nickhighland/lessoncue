## App name

LessonCue

## Short description (74 characters)

Self-hosted lesson presentation and media playback for classrooms and venues.

## Full description

LessonCue keeps presentations, videos, audio, images, slides, and online media organized and ready when the room needs them. Build a dated lesson in a browser served by your own local server, pair compatible Android TV, Google TV, or Fire TV displays, and prepare media ahead of time for dependable playback.

Plan a full class or event sequence with pre-roll media, countdowns, video trims, audio and video fades, repeating items, and cue-by-cue controls. Use a phone, tablet, or browser controller to select media, play, pause, seek, and move through the lesson. LessonCue is designed for classrooms, training rooms, worship spaces, educational facilities, and live presenters who prefer to keep their media and data on their own server.

This closed-test version contains one Sample Lesson with three slides. It demonstrates lesson selection and forward/backward navigation using a touchscreen, on-screen controls, or an Android TV, Google TV, or Fire TV remote. This test build does not connect to a LessonCue server.

LessonCue is free to self-host and use for non-commercial purposes. Commercial use requires a separate license.
