# Fire TV store assets

These files match the Fire TV upload form shown in the product brief:

| Upload field | File | Dimensions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| App icon | `app-icon-1280x720.png` | 1280 × 720 PNG |
| Background image | `background-1920x1080.png` | 1920 × 1080 PNG |
| Featured content logo | `featured-content-logo-640x260.png` | 640 × 260 PNG |
| Featured content background | `featured-content-background-1920x720.png` | 1920 × 720 PNG |
| Screenshot 1 | `screenshots/library-1920x1080.png` | 1920 × 1080 PNG |
| Screenshot 2 | `screenshots/lesson-1920x1080.png` | 1920 × 1080 PNG |
| Screenshot 3 | `screenshots/playback-1920x1080.png` | 1920 × 1080 PNG |

The three screenshots are native 1920 × 1080 captures from the LessonCue Android TV store build (0.40.57) running on a Google TV 1080p emulator. They show the real library, lesson-detail, and activity playback screens; they are not mockups or upscaled artwork. The non-screenshot fields use separate store-branding artwork, with their source masters retained under `source/` for future revisions.
